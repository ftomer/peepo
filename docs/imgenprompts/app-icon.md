# App icon

The store icon and the launcher icon, one square master image per concept.
Peepo the owl is the brand ([../branding.md](../branding.md)); the icon has to say
"peek and find" at 40 pixels, so it is one big shape, not a scene.

Style is the shared block from [art-direction.md](art-direction.md), with the icon
rules on top: no text, no letters, no numbers, nothing thinner than the outline,
and no detail that dies below 40px.

```style
Flat 2D vector cartoon illustration for a children's game.
Thick uniform dark outlines, big simple rounded shapes, flat cel shading with a single
soft shadow tone, bright saturated primary colours, cheerful and friendly.
No gradients, no texture, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism.
```

## Icon 3 owl and magnifier

```prompt
Flat 2D vector cartoon illustration for a children's game app icon.
Thick uniform dark outlines, big simple rounded shapes, flat cel shading with a single
soft shadow tone, bright saturated primary colours, cheerful and friendly.
No gradients, no texture, no painterly brushwork, no photorealism.

Centred composition on a flat sunny yellow background: one friendly round teal owl's
head, complete and uncropped, holding a chunky bright red magnifying glass up in front
of its left eye with a small blue wing, so that eye looks comically huge through the
thick round lens. The other eye is a big round white eye with a black pupil. Bright
yellow beak between the eyes. Small rounded ear tufts on top.

The whole owl, the whole magnifier and the whole handle sit inside the middle 75
percent of the square, perfectly centred, with a wide even margin of empty yellow
background on all four sides and in every corner. Nothing touches or crosses the edge
of the image. Nothing is cut off.

Bold and simple, readable when shrunk to a thumbnail. Square 1:1 composition, single
subject, no scene, no text, no letters, no numbers, no watermark, no border, no frame.
```
