# Peepo the mascot

Peepo the owl is the guide the kids direction calls for ([../kids-direction.md](../kids-direction.md)), and the face of the brand ([../branding.md](../branding.md)).
The app icon is his portrait; these are the poses the screens use.

Same style block as every other piece of art in the game, plus the mascot rules: one owl, no props except the ones named, no ground, no shadow, and a wide flat background the cutter can strip.

Same owl every time: rounded teal body, cream belly, big round white eyes with black pupils, bright yellow beak, small rounded ear tufts, thick dark navy outline.
The one thing that must never drift is his colour: Peepo is teal, never green ([../branding.md#app-icon](../branding.md#app-icon) says why).

`tools/scene.sh mascot` cuts each of these out onto transparency and writes `assets/peepo/<pose>.png`, which is what [lib/game/peepo.dart](../../lib/game/peepo.dart) draws.
