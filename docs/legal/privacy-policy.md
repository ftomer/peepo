# Privacy Policy for Peepo

Last updated: 23 August 2026.

Peepo is a hidden object game for children.
It is built so that there is nothing to say here beyond the short version, and the short version is this: **Peepo collects nothing, sends nothing, and stores everything it needs on your own device.**

## What we collect

Nothing.

Peepo has no accounts, no sign-in, no registration and no profiles.
We do not ask for a name, an email address, a phone number, a birthday, a photo, a contact list or a location.
We do not collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers or usage analytics.

## What the app stores on the device

Three things, and only on the device the app is installed on:

- the age band a grown-up chose,
- whether extra help is switched on,
- which levels have been finished.

These are written with the operating system's standard app-preferences storage.
They never leave the device, they are not backed up to us because there is no "us" to back them up to, and deleting the app deletes them.

## Network use

Peepo does not use the network.
The app has no code that opens a network connection, contains no third-party software development kits, and works exactly the same in aeroplane mode.

## Advertising and tracking

There is no advertising in Peepo, of any kind, from anybody.
There is no tracking, no third-party analytics, no attribution software and no remarketing.
Nothing in the app profiles a child or an adult.

## Children's privacy

Peepo is directed to children and is designed to comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), the UK Age Appropriate Design Code and the GDPR's rules on children's data.
Because the app collects no personal information from anybody, there is no children's data for a parent to review, correct or delete, and no verifiable parental consent to give.

Every decision meant for a grown-up - changing the age band, resetting progress, or following any link out of the app - sits behind a parental gate.

## Purchases

Peepo contains no in-app purchases.
If purchases are ever added, they will sit behind the parental gate, and this policy will be updated before that version ships.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "last updated" date above changes with it, and the updated policy is published at the same address.
Any change that affects data handling will be described plainly here rather than buried.
