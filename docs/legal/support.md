# Peepo support

Peepo is a hidden object game for children aged 3 to 12.
Tap what you spot, find everything Peepo has lost, and start the next room.

## Common questions

**How do I change the age band?**
Tap the age badge in the top right of the room list, answer the arithmetic question that appears, and pick one of the four bands.
The question is there so that the child playing does not end up in the settings.

**What do the bands change?**
How many things a room hides, how large they are, how close together they sit, how forgiving a tap is, and how quickly Peepo helps when nothing has been found for a while.
Peek is for ages 3 to 4, Look for 5 to 7, Seek for 8 to 10 and Hunt for 11 to 12.

**My child is stuck.**
They do not have to ask for help.
If nothing has been found for a while, Peepo points at something still hidden, and if that happens twice in a row the game quietly becomes more forgiving for the rest of the room.

**Can I start over?**
Yes. The grown-up settings, behind the same gate as the age band, have "Start the levels over".

**Does Peepo work without internet?**
Yes, completely. The app never uses the network.

**Does Peepo collect any data about my child?**
No. Nothing is collected and nothing leaves the device.
See the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.md).

**Is there advertising or anything to buy?**
No advertising, ever.
There is nothing to buy.
